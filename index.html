02 / About me

Always learning.
Always building.

I’m Kevin Long, a computer science student who enjoys using code to turn questions, ideas, and everyday problems into working projects.

Python is one of the main tools I use to learn and build. I enjoy its clear, flexible approach and use it to strengthen my understanding of programming fundamentals, problem solving, and how software is designed from the ground up.

I also code with AI as a collaborative tool. I use it to explore unfamiliar concepts, think through different approaches, debug more thoughtfully, and move from an early idea to a stronger implementation. For me, working with AI is not a substitute for understanding the code—it is a way to ask better questions, learn faster, and spend more time experimenting.

I’m interested in the full development journey: defining a useful problem, creating an intuitive experience, writing and refining the code, and learning from the finished result. I continue to grow by shipping personal projects, studying how strong products work, and taking on technical challenges that push me beyond what I already know.

I’m looking for opportunities where I can contribute my curiosity and perspective, learn from experienced teammates, and grow into a developer who builds dependable, useful software.
